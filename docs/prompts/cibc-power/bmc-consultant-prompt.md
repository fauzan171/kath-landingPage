## Tasks

### Task 1: Competition Structure Design

Design complete competition flow:

#### Phases
1. **Registration & Submission** (6-8 weeks)
   - What documents required?
   - What information needed?
   - Team size limits?
   - Eligibility criteria per category?

2. **Screening & Selection** (2-3 weeks)

   - Content evaluation method
   - How many teams advance?
   - Tiebreaker rules?

3. **Semifinal** (2-3 weeks prep + 1 week event)
   - Virtual or in-person?
   - Pitch duration?
   - Q&A duration?
   - Number of judges?

4. **Final Event** (1 month prep + 2-3 days)
   - Event format (in-person recommended)
   - Pitch format
   - Awards ceremony
   - Networking opportunities

#### Deliverable
Create detailed timeline with:
- Phase durations
- Key milestones
- Decision points
- Communication touchpoints

## Example Output

### Example: Innovation Scoring Rubric

```markdown
## Innovation & Creativity (25 points total)

### Novelty of Approach (8 points)

**8-7 points - Exceptional:**
- Completely new business model
- Challenges industry conventions
- First-to-market or blue ocean
- Example: Airbnb's home-sharing model

**6-5 points - Strong:**
- Significant improvement on existing models
- Novel combination of elements
- Clear differentiation from competitors
- Example: Uber's on-demand model applied to new industry

**4-3 points - Moderate:**
- Some innovative elements
- Adaptation of existing model to new market
- Incremental improvements
- Example: Food delivery service with unique cuisine focus

**2-1 points - Limited:**
- Minor variations of existing businesses
- Follows proven model closely
- Little differentiation
- Example: Another coffee shop

**0 points - None:**
- Direct copy of existing business
- No innovation evident
```
