## 📋 Business Flow - International Competition Standard

### Phase 1: Pre-Launch (4-6 weeks before opening)

#### Strategic Planning
- Define competition theme & categories
- Set prize structure & judging criteria
- Recruit judges & mentors
- Prepare legal framework
- Setup technology infrastructure

#### Marketing Preparation
- Branding & visual identity
- Website & landing page
- Social media strategy
- Press release preparation
- Partner outreach

---

### Phase 2: Registration & Submission (6-8 weeks)

#### Registration Flow
```
1. Participant visits landing page
2. Reads competition info & requirements
3. Creates account (email verification)
4. Completes profile
5. Forms team (optional, max 4 members)
6. Selects competition category
7. Submits initial BMC
8. Receives confirmation email
```

#### Required Documents
1. **Business Model Canvas** (1 page, PDF)
2. **Executive Summary** (1-2 pages, PDF)
3. **Pitch Deck** (10-15 slides, PDF)
4. **Video Pitch** (2-3 minutes, YouTube/Vimeo link)
5. **Team Profile** (completed in dashboard)

---

### Phase 3: Screening & Selection (2-3 weeks)

#### Administrative Review
- Verify document completeness
- Check eligibility criteria
- Validate team composition
- Confirm submission format

#### Content Evaluation
**Scoring Rubric:**
| Criteria | Weight |
|----------|--------|
| Innovation & Creativity | 25% |
| Market Potential | 20% |
| Business Model Viability | 25% |
| Team Capability | 15% |
| Presentation Quality | 15% |

#### Selection Process
1. Initial screening (pass/fail)
2. Scoring by judges (0-100 scale)
3. Top 20-30 teams advance to semifinal
4. Announcement of semifinalists

---

### Phase 4: Semifinal (2-3 weeks preparation, 1 week event)

#### Format Options
**Option A: Virtual Pitch**
- 10-minute pitch + 10-minute Q&A
- Recorded or live via Zoom
- Judges in different locations

**Option B: In-Person Regional**
- Regional hubs in multiple cities
- 50-100 participants per hub
- Local judges + remote head judges

#### Deliverables
- Refined pitch deck
- 3-minute video pitch
- Financial projection (1 page)
- Customer validation evidence

---

### Phase 5: Final Event (1 month preparation, 2-3 days event)

#### Final Event Structure

**Day 1: Welcome & Networking**
- Registration & welcome kit
- Opening ceremony
- Keynote speakers
- Networking session
- Team dinner

**Day 2: Competition**
- Morning: Finalist pitches (5-7 minutes each)
- Afternoon: Q&A with judges
- Evening: Awards gala & celebration

**Day 3: Post-Event** (Optional)
- Investor meetings
- Mentorship sessions
- Workshop & training
- Media interviews

#### Pitch Format
- **Pitch Duration:** 7 minutes
- **Q&A Duration:** 8 minutes
- **Total per team:** 15 minutes
- **Number of finalists:** 10-15 teams

---

### Phase 6: Post-Competition (Ongoing)

#### Winner Support
- Prize distribution
- Media coverage
- Investor introductions
- Mentorship program
- Incubation opportunities

#### Alumni Network
- Create competition alumni community
- Annual reunion events
- Ongoing mentorship
- Success story sharing

  country: z.string().min(1, 'Country required'),
  institutionType: z.enum(['student', 'startup', 'corporate']),
  teamName: z.string().optional(),
  agreeToTerms: z.boolean().refine(val => val === true, 'Must accept terms'),
});

export function RegistrationForm() {
