# CIBC Power by KATH - Competition Framework

## Overview

**Competition Name:** CIBC Power by KATH
**Theme:** Sustainability & Green Innovation

## Submission Requirements

### 1. Business Model Canvas (Required)

**9 Building Blocks:**
1. **Customer Segments** - Who are your target customers?
2. **Value Propositions** - What value do you deliver?
3. **Channels** - How do you reach customers?
4. **Customer Relationships** - How do you interact with customers?
5. **Revenue Streams** - How do you make money?
6. **Key Resources** - What assets do you need?
7. **Key Activities** - What must you do?
8. **Key Partnerships** - Who are your partners?
9. **Cost Structure** - What are your costs?

**Format:** PDF, A3 or A4 size
**Template:** Provided in resources section

### 2. Pitch Deck (Required)

**Slide Structure (10-12 slides):**
1. Title Slide - Project name, team, contact
2. Problem - What problem are you solving?
3. Solution - What is your solution?
4. Market Size - TAM, SAM, SOM
5. Business Model - How you make money
6. Go-to-Market - How you acquire customers
7. Competition - Competitive landscape
8. Team - Who's behind this?
9. Traction - What have you achieved?
10. Financials - Projections (3-5 years)
11. The Ask - What do you need?
12. Contact - How to reach you

**Format:** PDF or PPTX
**Max slides:** 15
**Recommended time:** 7-10 minutes presentation

### 3. Executive Summary (Required)

**Structure (1-2 pages):**
- Project Overview (1 paragraph)
- Problem Statement (1 paragraph)
- Solution (1 paragraph)
- Market Opportunity (1 paragraph)
- Business Model (1 paragraph)
- Team (1 paragraph)
- Financial Summary (1 paragraph)
- Current Status & Ask (1 paragraph)

**Format:** PDF or DOCX
**Word limit:** 500-1000 words

### 4. Video Pitch (Required)

**Requirements:**
- Duration: 2-3 minutes
- Platform: YouTube or Vimeo (unlisted)
- Quality: Minimum 720p
- Content: Problem, Solution, Team, Ask

**Tips:**
- Be authentic and enthusiastic
- Show your face/team
- Use simple language
- Practice before recording

## Judging Criteria

### Scoring System (100 points total)

#### 1. Innovation & Creativity (25 points)
**Criteria:**
- Novelty of approach (8 pts)
- Originality of solution (8 pts)
- Differentiation from existing solutions (9 pts)

**5 points - Exceptional:**
- Breakthrough innovation
- Completely new approach to problem
- Patent-worthy or unique IP

**4 points - Strong:**
- Significant improvement on existing solutions
- Novel combination of elements
- Clear market differentiation

**3 points - Good:**
- Some innovative elements
- Adaptation of existing model
- Moderate differentiation

**2 points - Fair:**
- Minor variations of existing solutions
- Limited differentiation

**1 point - Needs Improvement:**
- Lacks innovation
- Copies existing solutions

#### 2. Market Potential (20 points)
**Criteria:**
- Market size and growth (7 pts)
- Target customer clarity (6 pts)
- Go-to-market strategy (7 pts)

#### 3. Business Model Viability (25 points)
**Criteria:**
- Revenue model clarity (8 pts)
- Scalability potential (8 pts)
- Financial projections (9 pts)

#### 4. Sustainability Impact (15 points)
**Criteria:**
- Environmental impact (5 pts)
- Social impact (5 pts)
- SDG alignment (5 pts)

#### 5. Team Capability (10 points)
**Criteria:**
- Relevant experience (4 pts)
- Team composition (3 pts)
- Commitment level (3 pts)

#### 6. Presentation Quality (5 points)
**Criteria:**
- Clarity of communication (2 pts)
- Visual presentation (2 pts)
- Q&A handling (1 pt)

## Judge Guidelines

### Judge Responsibilities
1. Evaluate assigned submissions objectively
2. Provide constructive feedback
3. Declare conflicts of interest
4. Maintain confidentiality
5. Complete scoring within deadline

### Evaluation Process
1. Review submission materials
2. Score each criterion
3. Provide written feedback
4. Attend Q&A session (semifinals/finals)
5. Participate in deliberation

### Conflict of Interest
Judges must recuse themselves if:
- Personal relationship with team member
- Financial interest in project
- Same institution as participant
- Previous mentorship of team

### Red Flags to Watch
- Unrealistic financial projections
- No customer validation
- Copy of existing business
- Weak team dynamics
- Vague problem statement
- No clear sustainability impact

### Green Flags to Look For
- Deep understanding of problem
- Evidence of customer interviews
- Realistic milestones
- Committed team
- Clear value proposition
- Measurable impact metrics
